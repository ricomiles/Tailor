- (decision) Q2 resolved (miles): tag extraction is a closed controlled vocabulary derived from canon, not open-ended keyword extraction. Vocabulary is the union of every tags[] across work bullets, summaries, and skills items, plus a hand-maintained alias map for surface variants; extraction is a case-folded word-boundary scan. Rationale: a JD term with no canon counterpart cannot contribute to overlap, so an open extractor produces terms the score must discard, and it would be non-deterministic. Rejected TF-IDF/RAKE plus fuzzy mapping (noise, non-determinism) and local embeddings (adds a model dependency to the queue, which must populate instantly with no model call).
- (decision) One extractor serves both consumers. Queue score (CAP-1): tags from posting title and description, intersected with canon tags, scored as the sum of the max bullet weight carrying each matched tag, normalized 0-100, title hits weighted above body hits. Closest real experience (CAP-4): same extractor over the rejected bullet text, canon bullets ranked by tag overlap and tie-broken on weight, with a highest-weight-recent-role fallback so the panel is never empty.
